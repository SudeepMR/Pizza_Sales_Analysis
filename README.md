# Pizza Sales Analysis | Power BI Business Intelligence Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing pizza sales data using Power BI to generate actionable business insights related to revenue performance, customer ordering behavior, product trends, and operational KPIs.

The objective of the project was to transform raw transactional sales data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards and visual storytelling to support business decision-making.

The project demonstrates:
- Sales performance analysis
- KPI tracking
- Product and category analysis
- Revenue trend analysis
- Customer ordering behavior analysis
- Dashboard development using Power BI
- Business reporting and visualization

## Key Insights from the Dashboard

- The average number of customers per day is 60, and 12 p.m. to 1 p.m. is the peak sales hour
- There is an average of 2 pizzas per order, indicating consistent multi-item purchasing behavior
- Thai Chicken Pizza emerged as the best-selling pizza based on sales performance
- Total yearly revenue generated was approximately $817.86K
- Customers showed higher preference for Thai Chicken and Spicy Italian pizzas
- Brie Carre Pizza and Green Garden Pizza were identified as the least popular menu items
- Weekend and evening periods showed higher overall sales activity

---

## Business Recommendations

- Increase promotional focus on top-performing pizzas such as Thai Chicken and Spicy Italian
- Optimize staffing and inventory planning during peak business hours
- Consider removing or redesigning low-performing menu items
- Introduce combo offers and targeted promotions during high-demand periods
- Utilize customer ordering trends for operational and marketing strategy planning
